Plumbing Help in Highland Park

Highland Park is a Pflugerville community, not the Dallas area of the same name. Guides record homes built by Capital Pacific and Lennar from 2005 to 2008, with newer sections by D.R. Horton as recently as 2018.

It has an amenity center with a pool, a basketball court, a soccer field, and a playscape, plus more than 100 acres of parkland with a hike and bike trail.

Location

Where Highland Park Is

Highland Park lies southwest of the SH 45 and SH 130 interchange, roughly two miles north of downtown Pflugerville. Heatherwilde Boulevard at Kingston Lacy Boulevard is one access point.

Stone Hill Town Center is nearby, and the neighboring community of Heatherwilde runs along the same boulevard.

The Homes

Homes of Two Different Ages

Because sections were built at different times, a Highland Park street from 2006 and one from 2017 can have homes of very different ages. We ask when your home was built and confirm by looking.

If the original water heater is still in a 2005 to 2008 home, it is now close to two decades old. That is worth checking even if it is still heating.

Good to Know

Practical Notes for Newer Homes

This is general guidance. High water pressure can wear on fixtures and appliance valves over time. If you hear banging pipes or see frequent small leaks, ask us to check the pressure.

Do not ignore a slow drain. A slow drain is usually a local clog, and it is cheaper to clear early than after it backs up.
