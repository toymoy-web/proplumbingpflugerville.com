Why It Happens

Why This Happens in Pflugerville

Water damage calls in Pflugerville most often follow a burst pipe, a slab leak that went undetected for a while, or a sump pump failure during a heavy storm. The restoration approach depends heavily on how long the water sat before it was discovered, since that determines how far moisture has spread into building materials.

Whether the water came from a failed line in Old Town or a slab leak in Falcon Pointe, the first hours decide how far moisture spreads into drywall and flooring.

Signs to Watch For

How to Tell You Need This

Visible Standing Water

Standing water needs to be extracted quickly to limit how far it spreads into flooring and subflooring.

Water Stains on Walls or Ceilings

A stain, even a dry one, often means moisture reached that area at some point and may still be trapped behind the surface.

Musty or Damp Smell

A persistent musty odor is often the first sign of moisture retained inside a wall cavity or under flooring.

Warped or Buckling Flooring

Flooring that's warped, cupped, or lifting is a sign moisture has already penetrated the material itself.

How It Works

Our Water Damage Restoration Process

The first priority is extracting standing water and identifying the full extent of moisture intrusion using moisture meters, which detect dampness inside walls and under flooring that isn't visible from the surface.

Once the affected area is mapped, industrial air movers and dehumidifiers dry the space over a period of days, with moisture readings taken periodically to confirm materials have actually dried through rather than just appearing dry on the surface.

Decision Guide

Which Option Fits Your Situation

How much material needs to be removed versus dried in place depends on how long the water was present and what it touched.

Dry in Place

  • Possible when water is caught early.
  • Preserves existing flooring and drywall.
  • Faster overall timeline.
  • Requires thorough moisture verification before closing up.

Remove and Replace Affected Material

  • Necessary when materials have absorbed water over time.
  • Reduces mold risk in porous materials like drywall and carpet padding.
  • Standard for water present more than 24-48 hours.
  • Followed by structural drying of the remaining space.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast do you respond to water damage?

Our emergency line for water damage is answered 24/7; the faster water is extracted, the less material typically needs to be replaced.

Will insurance cover this?

Sudden events like a burst pipe are often covered; a slow leak that went undetected for a while is more likely to be excluded, depending on your specific policy.

How do you know if the space is fully dry?

Moisture meter readings inside walls and under flooring confirm actual dryness, not just surface appearance.

Do you handle the plumbing repair too?

Restoration and the underlying plumbing repair are related but separate; our plumbing team can address the cause while restoration handles the resulting damage.

How long does the drying process usually take?

Most structural drying takes three to five days, depending on how much moisture the affected materials absorbed and how large the affected area is.
