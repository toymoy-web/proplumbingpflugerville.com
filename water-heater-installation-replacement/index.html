Why It Happens

Why This Happens in Pflugerville

Water heaters in Pflugerville tend to fail a few years earlier than the manufacturer's rated lifespan. The main reason is hard water delivered through the City of Pflugerville Water and Wastewater Utility and Manville Water Supply Corporation, which leaves mineral scale on the tank's heating element and along the bottom of the tank. That scale insulates the water from the heat source and makes the unit work harder for the same output.

Signs to Watch For

How to Tell You Need This

Age Over 10 Years

Most tank water heaters are rated for 8 to 12 years; past that, a repair is often a short-term fix on a unit near the end of its service life.

Rust-Colored Water

Rust in the hot water only, not the cold, usually means the tank itself is corroding from the inside.

Popping or Rumbling Sounds

Sediment hardened at the bottom of the tank causes a popping sound during heating cycles.

Water Pooling at the Base

A wet spot around the tank's base can mean a failing tank seam, which isn't repairable.

How It Works

Our Water Heater Install Process

For a tank replacement, we size the new unit to your household's actual usage rather than a like-for-like swap, since a family's hot water needs often change over the life of the old unit. For a tankless conversion, we check the existing gas line size and venting, since tankless units generally need a larger gas supply line than a standard tank.

Every installation includes removing and hauling away the old unit, setting the new one on a proper stand or pan where required by code, and pressure-testing all connections before we consider the job finished.

Decision Guide

Which Option Fits Your Situation

Tank and tankless each make sense in different situations, and neither is a universal upgrade.

Tank Replacement

  • Lower upfront cost.
  • Simpler installation, less rework.
  • Familiar maintenance.
  • Good fit for straightforward like-for-like swaps.

Tankless Conversion

  • Endless hot water, no tank to run out.
  • Smaller footprint.
  • Often needs a larger gas line and new venting.
  • Longer service life when properly maintained.
