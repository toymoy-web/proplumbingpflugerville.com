What Causes This in Pflugerville Homes

The City of Pflugerville Water and Wastewater Utility and Manville Water Supply Corporation both deliver water with a meaningful mineral content. That's why fixtures here develop white scale buildup faster than in many other Texas markets. Why water heaters in this area tend to fail a few years earlier than their rated lifespan.

Warning Signs

Signs You Need Water Softener

White Scale on Fixtures

Chalky white buildup around faucet aerators and showerheads is the most visible sign of hard water.

Soap That Won't Lather

Hard water reacts with soap to form a film rather than a lather, which is why skin can feel dry after showering.

Spotty Dishes and Glassware

Mineral spots on dishes even after a full dishwasher cycle point to hard water, not the dishwasher itself.

Faster Water Heater Wear

A water heater failing years ahead of schedule is often a downstream effect of untreated hard water.

Our Process

How We Diagnose and Fix It

We size a softener to your household's actual water usage and hardness level. An undersized unit runs constantly and wears out faster while an oversized one wastes salt and water on regeneration cycles you don't need.

Installation ties into the main water line ahead of the water heater, so the softened water benefits both the heater and every fixture in the house rather than treating only part of the system.

System Not Working Right?

If your softener isn't regenerating properly, isn't using salt, or the water still feels hard, that's usually a resin or valve issue we can diagnose and repair rather than replace outright.

Your Options

Repair vs. Replacement

Repair or replace depends mostly on the unit's age and whether the resin bed itself has failed.

Repair the Existing Unit

  • Makes sense for units under 8-10 years old.
  • Resin, valve, or timer issues are often fixable.
  • Lower upfront cost.
  • Extends the life of a working system.

Replace the Unit

  • Makes sense when the resin bed has fully failed.
  • Better fit for outdated or undersized systems.
  • Newer units regenerate more efficiently.
  • Sized correctly for your actual household usage.

FAQs

Common Questions

How do I know what size softener I need?

It comes down to household size and your water's actual hardness level, not a one-size-fits-all unit.

Why isn't my softener using salt?

That's usually a bridging issue in the salt tank or a valve problem, both of which are typically repairable.

Will a softener fix my water heater problems?

It won't undo existing scale damage, but it prevents new scale buildup that shortens water heater life going forward.

Do you install whole-house filtration alongside a softener?

Yes, the two systems work together and are often installed at the same time.

How much maintenance does a water softener need?

Mainly keeping the salt tank filled; beyond that, a well-installed system runs largely on its own between periodic checkups.
