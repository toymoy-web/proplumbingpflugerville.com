Areas We Serve Near Pflugerville, TX

Round Rock

Round Rock's water runs very hard, roughly 249 mg/L by the city's own reporting, which is a real factor in how fast local water heaters scale up. We dispatch across 78664, 78665, and 78681, from the older neighborhoods near downtown to newer developments like Teravista.

View Round Rock Page →

Hutto

Hutto is genuinely split between three separate retail water providers, the City of Hutto, Manville Water Supply Corporation, and Jonah Water Special Utility District, depending on the subdivision. We cover all of ZIP 78634, including Lakeside Estates, Riverwalk, and Hutto Highlands.

View Hutto Page →

Manor

Manor's water comes through a mix of EPCOR, Manville Water Supply Corporation, and the city's own wells, and not every neighborhood is a direct City of Manor customer. We serve ShadowGlen, Presidential Meadows, Stonewater, and the rest of ZIP 78653.

View Manor Page →

Wells Branch

Most of Wells Branch was built in the mid-1980s through the mid-1990s, which puts a large share of homes here in the galvanized and polybutylene pipe window we watch for on every call. Water and wastewater service runs through the Wells Branch MUD rather than the City of Austin directly.

View Wells Branch Page →

North Austin

This part of North Austin, centered on Milwood and Scofield Farms near ZIP 78727, is served directly by Austin Water. Milwood's original 1980s and early-1990s homes sit alongside newer, amenity-heavy development along the Parmer Lane tech corridor.

Local Signature

What Most of These Areas Have in Common

Two patterns show up across almost every town we cover. First, hard water: Central Texas groundwater moves through limestone formations that load it with calcium and magnesium, and Round Rock's own utility reports put local hardness at roughly 249 mg/L, high enough to meaningfully shorten water heater life across the whole area. Second, housing-era pipe material: neighborhoods built from the 1980s through the mid-1990s, common in Wells Branch, Milwood, and parts of Round Rock and Hutto, were plumbed with galvanized steel or polybutylene. Both materials are now reaching the point where a repair or a planned repipe becomes a real conversation. We factor both patterns into how we diagnose a call before we ever arrive, rather than treating every town the same way.
