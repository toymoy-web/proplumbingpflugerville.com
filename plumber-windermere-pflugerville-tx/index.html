Plumbing Help in Windermere

Windermere is one of the older residential areas in Pflugerville. Neighborhood guides describe it as built from the mid 1980s to the mid 1990s, with mostly single-story homes and mature trees.

It has a homeowners association, Windermere Homeowners Association, Inc. Sources also say there is no community pool. We work on the plumbing inside and around the home, not the common areas.

Location

Where Windermere Is

Windermere sits on the west side of Pflugerville off Grand Avenue Parkway, with York Castle Drive as one access street. One neighborhood guide frames the area with West Pflugerville Parkway, Gower Street, and Grand Avenue Parkway.

Sources disagree on exact edges, so we go by your address. Windermere uses ZIP 78660 like the rest of Pflugerville.

Good to Know

Practical Notes for Established Neighborhoods

This is general guidance. In any neighborhood with mature trees, roots can reach sewer lines over time. If a drain keeps slowing down after it has been cleared, a camera inspection can show whether the line itself needs attention.

Check your water heater label for its manufacture date and look for rust or moisture at the base. Catching a small leak early is usually cheaper than cleaning up after a failed tank.

FAQ

Windermere Plumbing Questions

Where is Windermere in Pflugerville?

On the west side of town off Grand Avenue Parkway, near York Castle Drive. If you are not sure whether your street counts as Windermere, give us the address and we will confirm service.

Is Windermere an older neighborhood?

Neighborhood guides describe it as built from the mid 1980s to the mid 1990s. Age alone does not tell us what is inside your walls, so we inspect before we recommend anything.

Do you handle townhome or duplex plumbing here?

Yes. For attached homes, check with your HOA or landlord about which lines they maintain, then call us for repairs on your side of the shutoff.

How do I know if my water heater is near the end of its life?

Look at the label for the manufacture date, and watch for rust, water at the base, or rumbling noises. If you see any of those, call and we will look at it.
