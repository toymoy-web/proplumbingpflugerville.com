The Historic Core

Plumbing Help in Old Town Pflugerville

Pflugerville grew up around this part of town. The town site was platted in 1904, and a general store built by Louis Bohls in 1890 is recorded as the first commercial business in the community. Early wooden buildings went up along Main Street and Pecan Street.

Texas Historical Commission records show the East Main Street Historic District was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2012. That listing covers a small group of buildings on East Main Street, not every property in the area.

Location

Where Old Town Sits

Local guides place Old Town around Pecan Street and Railroad Avenue, with Pflugerville Plaza, Heritage Park, and the Bohls House nearby. Bohls Place, a neighborhood built out in 2002 off Pecan Street, is close by.

Old Town has no official boundary that we rely on. When you call, we ask for your street address instead of guessing which side of the line a property falls on.

The Homes

A Mix of Buildings, Not One Subdivision

Old Town is a mixed district. Historic commercial buildings, small businesses, and residential streets are within walking distance of each other. That means calls here can be for a home, a shop, or an office.

We do not assume what is behind the walls of any particular building. Each visit starts with a look at the actual pipes, fixtures, and shutoff valves, and you get an upfront price before work begins.

Good to Know

Practical Notes for Older and Mixed-Use Buildings

This is general plumbing guidance, not a claim about any specific building. In older buildings, shutoff valves can be hard to find or stiff from lack of use. Locate yours before you need it and turn it once to make sure it moves.

If you rent, ask the building owner which valve controls your unit. Tell us whether the property is a home, shop, or office when you call, because that changes what we bring.

FAQ

Old Town Plumbing Questions

What area does Old Town Pflugerville cover?

Informally, the streets around East Main Street, Pecan Street, and Railroad Avenue, near Pflugerville Plaza and Heritage Park. There is no official line we rely on, so we confirm service by address.

Do you serve businesses in Old Town as well as homes?

Yes. We handle residential plumbing, and our commercial plumbing page covers business calls. Tell us whether the property is a home, shop, or office when you call.

Who should I call first if I am a tenant with a leak?

Tell your landlord or building manager, and shut off the nearest valve if water is actively flowing. If you are authorized to request service, call us and we will explain what we find.

Can you find a hidden leak in an older building?

Our leak detection page explains how we locate hidden leaks before opening a wall or floor. Call with what you are seeing, such as a damp spot or a high water bill, and we will advise the next step.
