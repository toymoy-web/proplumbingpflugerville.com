Plumbing Help in Heatherwilde

Heatherwilde was built in sections rather than all at once. Neighborhood guides describe development running from the late 1980s into the early 2000s, and listing records show build years from 1986 to 2003.

One guide names Centex, Buffington, and Hampton as builders. Homes range from about 1,200 to 3,200 square feet, so a neighbor two streets over may have a different layout and different fixtures.

Location

Where Heatherwilde Is

Heatherwilde lies near the intersection of Heatherwilde Boulevard and Pflugerville Parkway, on both sides of North Heatherwilde Boulevard south of East Pflugerville Parkway.

It has a neighborhood pool, a park and playground, tennis courts, and trails. Gilleland Creek Park, Lake Pflugerville, and Stone Hill Town Center are all a short drive away.

The Homes

Several Sections, Several Ages

Guides describe several sections, including Estates of Heatherwilde, and note that HOA coverage is not the same in every section. Because the neighborhood was built over many years, we ask which street you are on and when the home was built.

That tells us what to expect, but we still confirm by looking. Updates by previous owners are common in any neighborhood this size.

Good to Know

Practical Notes for Multi-Section Neighborhoods

This is general guidance. If a home has had owners over the years, ask what was replaced and when. Receipts for a water heater or a repipe can save time and money when something fails.

Know where your main water shutoff is before you need it. Turn it off and on once a year so the valve does not seize.

FAQ

Heatherwilde Plumbing Questions

Is Heatherwilde one neighborhood or several?

It was built in sections over many years, and Estates of Heatherwilde is one named section. Give us your street and we will confirm we serve it.

Does home age change what you check?

We check what is actually installed instead of assuming. Homes built across the late 1980s to early 2000s may include original fixtures or later upgrades, and we explain what we find.

What should I do if my water heater is leaking?

Turn off power or gas to the heater, close the cold water supply valve on top, and call us. Do not wait if water is spreading.

Do you also serve Highland Park next door?

Yes. Highland Park is a separate community that also uses Heatherwilde Boulevard for access, and it has its own page.
