Know Your Water

What's In Hutto's Water

Hutto is genuinely split between three separate retail water providers depending on the subdivision. The City of Hutto runs its own wells into the Carrizo-Wilcox aquifer, Manville Water Supply Corporation serves neighborhoods like Lakeside Estates and Riverwalk, and Jonah Water Special Utility District serves areas including Hutto Highlands and Mustang Creek. Which one serves your address affects who to call for a water-supply issue outside your home, though it doesn't change how we diagnose or repair your interior plumbing.

Groundwater in this part of Williamson County runs hard, though Hutto doesn't publish one single blended hardness figure for the whole city the way some neighboring utilities do.
