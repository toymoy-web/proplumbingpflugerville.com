What Causes This in Pflugerville Homes

The same hard water that scales a standard tank builds up even faster inside a tankless unit's narrow heat exchanger passages. The water moves through a much smaller surface area at higher velocity. Without periodic descaling, a tankless unit in this area can lose noticeable output within a couple of years of installation.

Warning Signs

Signs You Need Tankless Water Heater

Reduced Hot Water Flow

A drop in flow rate or output temperature is often the first sign of scale buildup restricting the heat exchanger.

Unit Won't Fire Consistently

Intermittent ignition problems can point to a sensor or igniter issue, separate from scale buildup.

Error Code on the Display

Most tankless units display a specific error code that narrows down the cause before we even arrive.

No Service Since Installation

A unit that's never had a descaling flush, especially after two or more years, is due for maintenance regardless of symptoms.

Our Process

How We Diagnose and Fix It

Descaling involves circulating a mild acidic solution through the unit's heat exchanger using a pump and hose kit connected to the service valves, which dissolves accumulated mineral scale without disassembling the unit.

For an error code or ignition issue, we read the specific code the manufacturer's unit displays, which usually points directly to a sensor, igniter, or venting problem rather than requiring open-ended troubleshooting.

Your Options

Repair vs. Replacement

Regular descaling extends a tankless unit's life significantly, and it's a different maintenance mindset than a tank unit needs.

Skip Regular Descaling

  • No upfront maintenance cost.
  • Scale buildup reduces output over time.
  • Increases risk of premature heat exchanger failure.
  • Often more expensive to fix once output has dropped.

Annual Descaling Service

  • Maintains full output and efficiency.
  • Extends heat exchanger lifespan.
  • Catches early sensor or venting issues.
  • Recommended yearly in hard water areas like Pflugerville.

FAQs

Common Questions

How often should a tankless unit be descaled in Pflugerville?

Annually is a reasonable standard given the area's hard water, though households with a water softener installed ahead of the unit can often go longer between services.

What does an error code on my unit mean?

It's specific to the manufacturer, but it generally narrows the problem to a sensor, ignition, or venting issue, which we diagnose directly from the code.

Can I switch from a tank to a tankless unit?

Yes, though it typically requires a larger gas line and different venting; see our water heater installation and replacement page for the comparison.

Does a water softener reduce how often I need to descale?

Yes, softened water significantly slows the mineral buildup that descaling addresses.

Is it worth switching from a tank to a tankless unit for the energy savings alone?

Energy savings vary by household usage; tankless units heat water on demand rather than keeping a full tank hot around the clock, which tends to help most in higher-usage households.
