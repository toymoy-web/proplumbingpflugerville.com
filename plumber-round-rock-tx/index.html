Before We Arrive

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak

The first minute matters most with a burst pipe. Here's what to do while you're on the phone with us.

Shut Off the Main Valve

Reach the main valve and turn it clockwise to stop water at the source.

Kill Power to Wet Areas

Flip the breaker for any room where water is pooling near an outlet.

Clear the Area

Pull rugs and furniture back from the wet area if it's safe to do so.

Local Water

Water & Utility Notes for Round Rock

Round Rock's water supply is a blend of surface water from Lake Georgetown and groundwater from the Edwards Aquifer. The system carries a 'Superior' rating from the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality.

The city's own water quality reports put average hardness at roughly 249 mg/L, or about 14.5 grains per gallon, which is considered very hard water and a real driver of scale buildup in tank water heaters and fixtures.
