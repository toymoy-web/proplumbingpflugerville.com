The Pflugerville Factor

Pflugerville's municipal water supply meets safety standards. From Villages of Hidden Lake to Old Town, taste, odor, and sediment complaints are what bring most Pflugerville homeowners to a whole-home filtration system.

Do You Need This?

Do You Need Water Filtration?

Chlorine Taste or Smell

A noticeable chlorine taste, especially right at the tap, is one of the most common reasons households request filtration.

Visible Sediment

Fine sediment in a glass of water, or clouding that clears from the bottom up, points to particulate that a sediment filter addresses directly.

Relying on Bottled Water at Home

If your household buys bottled water specifically because tap water tastes off, that's usually a sign filtration would solve it at the source.

Different Symptoms Than Hardness

Taste and odor issues are a separate problem from hard water scale, which is what a softener addresses instead.

Our Approach

How We Handle It

We install whole-home filtration at the point where the main line enters the house, so every fixture benefits rather than just a single under-sink filter. We size the filter media (carbon, sediment, or a combination) to the specific taste or particulate issue you're actually experiencing.

For households wanting both softened and filtered water, the two systems are plumbed in sequence, typically filtration first to protect the softener's resin bed from sediment, followed by the softener itself.

Your Choices

What Your Options Look Like

Filtration and softening solve different problems, and knowing which one you actually need avoids paying for the wrong system.

Whole-Home Filtration

  • Addresses taste, odor, and sediment.
  • Doesn't require salt or regeneration cycles.
  • Protects fixtures and appliances from particulate.
  • Often paired with a softener, not a replacement for one.

Water Softener

  • Addresses hardness and mineral scale specifically.
  • Requires salt and periodic regeneration.
  • Protects water heater and pipes from scale buildup.
  • Doesn't resolve taste or odor issues.

Answers

Questions We Hear Often

Is my Pflugerville tap water actually unsafe?

Municipal water here meets safety standards; filtration is generally about taste, odor, and sediment preference, not a safety concern.

Do I need both a filter and a softener?

Only if you have both taste/sediment complaints and hardness issues; many households only need one or the other.

How often do filter cartridges need to be replaced?

It depends on your household's water usage and the filter type, typically every few months to a year.

Will filtration reduce my water pressure?

A properly sized system shouldn't cause a noticeable pressure drop; undersizing the filter housing is the usual cause when that happens.

Can filtration remove a chlorine smell completely?

A carbon filter stage typically resolves chlorine taste and odor very effectively, since carbon is specifically effective at adsorbing the compounds responsible for it.

How long does a whole-home filtration system last?

A properly maintained system with regular cartridge changes typically performs well for many years before the housing or media itself needs replacing.
