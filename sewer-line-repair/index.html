Why It Happens

Why This Happens in Pflugerville

Expansive clay soil is the leading cause of sewer line trouble in Pflugerville's newer developments. The same seasonal soil movement that stresses supply lines also puts pressure on the sewer line running from the house to the street connection. In more established neighborhoods, tree root intrusion into aging clay or cast iron sewer pipe is the more common culprit, particularly where mature trees sit close to the line's path.

Signs to Watch For

How to Tell You Need This

Recurring Backups

A backup that clears with snaking and returns within weeks usually means a structural problem in the line, not just debris.

Soggy or Unusually Green Patch in the Yard

A consistently wet or extra-green area over the sewer line's path can mean it's leaking into the surrounding soil.

Multiple Drains Backing Up at Once

Every fixture backing up together, especially the lowest drain in the house, points to the main sewer line rather than an individual fixture.

Sewage Odor Outdoors

A sewage smell near the yard's cleanout or the line's path can mean a crack has developed.

How It Works

Our Sewer Line Repair Process

A sewer camera inspection is the starting point for almost every sewer line call. It shows exactly where a crack, offset joint, or root intrusion sits along the line's length, which determines whether the fix is a localized spot repair or a longer section replacement.

For root intrusion specifically, hydro jetting clears the roots from the pipe interior; if the roots have already cracked the pipe rather than just growing through a joint, that section needs to be replaced rather than just cleared.

Decision Guide

Which Option Fits Your Situation

The right fix depends heavily on what the camera actually shows, not just which symptom brought us out.

Trenchless Spot Repair

  • Fixes a localized crack or offset joint.
  • Less yard disruption than a full dig.
  • Faster turnaround.
  • Makes sense when the rest of the line tests sound.

Section Replacement

  • Needed when a length of pipe is structurally compromised.
  • Addresses recurring root intrusion at the source.
  • More durable fix for older clay or cast iron pipe.
  • Camera inspection confirms the exact extent needed.
Cost Factors

What Determines the Price

Sewer line repair pricing depends on the pipe's depth, the extent of the damage, and whether a trenchless method is a viable option.

Depth and AccessA shallower line under open yard is faster to reach than one under a driveway or foundation.
Damage ExtentA single offset joint costs less to fix than a collapsed or root-compromised section.
Pipe MaterialOlder clay or cast iron sometimes needs different repair methods than modern PVC.
Trenchless EligibilityNot every situation qualifies for trenchless repair; the camera inspection confirms this upfront.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to dig up my whole yard for a sewer repair?

Not always. Trenchless spot repair is often possible for a localized crack or joint issue, based on what the camera inspection shows.

Are tree roots really strong enough to crack a sewer pipe?

Roots typically grow into a pipe through an existing small crack or joint gap rather than cracking solid pipe outright, then widen the opening as they grow.

How often should I have my sewer line inspected?

If you've had one backup already, a camera inspection is worth doing before it becomes a repeat problem; otherwise there's no fixed schedule for a line with no history of issues.

Can hydro jetting fix root intrusion permanently?

It clears the immediate blockage, but if the roots are entering through a crack, the crack itself typically still needs repair to prevent regrowth.
