Local Causes

Local Causes in Pflugerville

Expansive clay soil expands when it absorbs moisture and contracts when it dries out. That repeated seasonal movement puts real stress on the supply and drain lines running underneath a home's slab. Over years of these cycles, a pipe joint or a section of pipe can crack or separate, and because it's under the concrete, the leak often isn't discovered until water finds its way up through the slab or the water bill spikes.

Expansive clay moves under newer slab homes in Falcon Pointe and Blackhawk and under older ones in Highland Park, which is why a warm spot on the floor deserves a prompt check.

Recognizing the Problem

Signs Slab Leak Repair Can't Wait

Warm Spot on the Floor

A hot water line leak under the slab often creates a noticeably warm patch on the floor above it.

Unexplained Rise in Water Bill

A slab leak can run continuously and go unnoticed for a long stretch, showing up first as a billing anomaly.

Sound of Running Water With Everything Off

If every fixture is off and you can still hear water running, a slab leak is one of the most likely explanations.

Cracks in Flooring or Baseboards

Water pressure building under the slab can eventually cause visible cracking in tile, flooring, or nearby baseboards.

The Fix

How the Fix Actually Works

We confirm a suspected slab leak using acoustic listening equipment and thermal imaging. Thermal imaging reads the temperature difference a hot water leak leaves in the concrete, letting us narrow the leak's location to a specific area of the slab before any concrete is disturbed.

Once located, the repair options are a direct access repair, cutting into the slab at the confirmed leak point, or a re-route that runs a new line through the attic or an accessible wall path instead, bypassing the damaged section under the slab entirely.

Warm Spot That Won't Explain Itself?

A warm patch on the floor with no obvious source, combined with a rising water bill, is one of the clearest signals of a slab leak our thermal imaging equipment is built to confirm.

Weighing Options

Choosing the Right Fix

The right repair method depends on the leak's location under the slab and how accessible a re-route path would be.

Direct Slab Access Repair

  • Repairs the exact failed section.
  • Makes sense for a single, accessible leak point.
  • More disruptive to flooring at the repair site.
  • Fastest option when access is straightforward.

Re-Route Through Attic or Wall

  • Avoids cutting into the slab entirely.
  • Better option when the leak sits under load-bearing areas.
  • Can address a broader corrosion pattern at once.
  • Leaves the original leak point permanently isolated.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How urgent is a slab leak?

It should be addressed promptly, since ongoing water under the slab can affect the foundation and flooring over time, though it's rarely an immediate structural emergency the way a burst pipe is.

Will you have to break up my whole floor?

Not necessarily; thermal imaging narrows the leak to a specific spot, and a re-route option can sometimes avoid the slab entirely.

Is a slab leak covered by insurance?

Often the detection and access costs are covered, though policies vary; check your specific homeowners policy language.

Why is this more common in Pflugerville than elsewhere?

The area's expansive clay soil moves more with seasonal moisture changes than sandier or more stable soil types, which puts more stress on under-slab plumbing over time.
