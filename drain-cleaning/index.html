What Causes This in Pflugerville Homes

Pflugerville homes built in the 1980s and 1990s often still run galvanized drain lines. Decades of mineral scale and corrosion inside that pipe narrow the usable diameter until ordinary hair and grease finally seal it shut.

Newer homes have a different problem: expansive clay soil under the slab shifts with rainfall and drought cycles. That movement can offset or crack the drain line itself, which shows up as a drain that clogs again within weeks of being snaked.

On the older streets around Old Town and Highland Park, buildup inside aging drain lines is the usual culprit. In newer Blackhawk and Falcon Pointe sections, a clog is more often a one-off blockage than a worn pipe.

Warning Signs

Signs You Need Drain Cleaning

Slow Drainage in Multiple Fixtures

Water backing up in more than one sink or tub at once usually points to the main line, not a single fixture trap.

Gurgling Sounds

A gurgling toilet when you run the washing machine or shower is a classic sign of a partial main line blockage.

Recurring Clogs

A drain that clears with a store-bought snake and clogs again within a month usually has a structural cause, not just debris.

Sewage Odor Indoors

A persistent sewage smell near a floor drain or cleanout can mean a crack or break, not just a clog.

Our Process

How We Diagnose and Fix It

For a straightforward fixture clog, we start with a mechanical drain snake sized to the pipe. For anything that keeps coming back, we run a sewer camera through the line first, which shows us exactly where the blockage sits and whether it's grease, roots, a collapsed section, or scale buildup rather than guessing.

Once we know what we're dealing with, hydro jetting clears grease and mineral buildup off the interior pipe wall using high-pressure water, which lasts longer than snaking alone because it scours the whole diameter instead of just punching a hole through the clog.

Your Options

Repair vs. Replacement

Not every clog needs the same fix, and pushing a snake through a structural problem just delays the real repair.

DIY Store-Bought Snake

  • Only reaches a few feet into the line.
  • Can't clear grease coating or root intrusion.
  • Risks scratching porcelain or older galvanized pipe.
  • No way to confirm the actual cause.

Professional Camera + Hydro Jetting

  • Camera confirms the real cause before we touch it.
  • Hydro jetting clears the full pipe diameter.
  • Identifies cracked or offset sections from clay soil movement.
  • Upfront price before any work starts.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can hydro jetting damage old pipes?

Galvanized and older clay pipe can be jetted at a lower pressure setting; we assess the pipe material from the camera inspection before choosing pressure.

How do I know if it's a fixture clog or a main line problem?

If more than one drain is slow or backing up at the same time, it's almost always the main line rather than an individual fixture trap.

Do you offer 24/7 drain cleaning?

Yes, our line is answered around the clock for backups and active overflow situations.

Will you show me what's actually in the line?

Yes, the camera inspection footage is available so you can see the actual blockage, not just take our word for it.
