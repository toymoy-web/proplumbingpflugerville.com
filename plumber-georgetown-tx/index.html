Before We Arrive

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak

Whether you're near the Square or out in Sun City, these first steps limit the damage while help is on the way.

Shut Off the Main Valve

Close the main shutoff, usually at the meter box or where the line enters the house.

Kill Power to Wet Areas

Turn off the breaker for any outlet or appliance sitting in or near the water.

Clear the Area

Lift rugs, boxes, and anything on the floor out of the wet zone if you safely can.
