Master-Planned Community

Plumbing Help in Falcon Pointe

Falcon Pointe is a master-planned community developed by Newland Communities. Sources record build-out beginning in January 2002 and builders active into 2017, with names including Lennar, Meritage, Centerra, and Chesmar.

Homes built in that window are now roughly a decade or two old. In general, water heaters, faucets, and toilets begin to reach replacement age in that range.

Location

Where Falcon Pointe Is

Falcon Pointe lies east of SH 130 near Kelly Lane and East Pflugerville Parkway, beside Blackhawk Golf Club. Sources describe the boundaries slightly differently, so we go by your address.

Guides describe pools, a fitness center, a bark park, trails, and Central Park with a disc golf course.

The Homes

Single-Family Homes and Condos

The community includes single-family homes under the Falcon Pointe Community Association and condominiums under a separate condominium association. That split matters when a leak starts in a shared wall or line.

If you live in a condo, check with your association about which lines it maintains. Then call us for repairs on your side, and we will tell you what we find.

Practical Notes for Newer Construction

This is general guidance. If a home or appliance is still under a builder or manufacturer warranty, check the terms before you hire anyone, because some warranties require specific service.

FAQ

Falcon Pointe Plumbing Questions

Do you service Falcon Pointe condos?

Yes. Check with the condominium association about which lines it maintains, then call us for repairs on your side of the shutoff.

How old are Falcon Pointe homes?

Sources record builders active from 2002 into 2017, so homes are roughly a decade or two old. Your water heater label will show when your unit was made.

Where is Falcon Pointe compared with Blackhawk?

They are neighbors in northeast Pflugerville, with Blackhawk Golf Club between and around them. Each has its own page.

Can you help if my builder warranty has expired?

Yes. Warranties vary, so check yours first. If it has lapsed, call us and we will explain the repair options and give an upfront price.
