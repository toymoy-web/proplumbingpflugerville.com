The Pflugerville Factor

The Local Factor Behind This Problem

Expansive clay soil movement is the most common cause of main water line failure in Pflugerville. The line runs underground across the full distance from the meter to the house and any section can be stressed as the surrounding soil shifts seasonally. Older galvanized service lines add a second failure mode, corroding from the inside even without any soil movement at all.

Do You Need This?

Do You Need Water Line Repair?

Low Pressure Throughout the House

A drop in pressure at every fixture, not just one, often points to a restriction or leak in the main line itself.

Unusually High Water Bill

A main line leak can lose a significant volume of water underground before it ever becomes visible above ground.

Wet Spot in the Yard Along the Line's Path

A consistently soggy area tracing the line's likely path from the meter to the house is a strong sign of an underground leak.

Discolored Water at Every Tap

Rust or sediment appearing at every fixture, rather than just one, points to the main line or service pipe rather than a single fixture issue.

Our Approach

How We Handle It

We start by testing pressure at the meter and at the house to confirm whether the loss is happening in the main line itself. Use acoustic leak detection equipment to pinpoint the leak's location along the line's underground path before any digging starts.

Where the situation allows, trenchless pipe bursting or pipe lining replaces the line with minimal excavation, pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one rather than digging a full trench the length of the yard.

Your Choices

What Your Options Look Like

Trenchless methods aren't always possible, and the right approach depends on the line's depth, material, and surrounding obstacles.

Trenchless Replacement

  • Minimal yard excavation.
  • Faster completion than traditional trenching.
  • Works well for straight-line runs.
  • May not be viable around certain obstacles.

Traditional Trench Replacement

  • Necessary when trenchless isn't viable.
  • Full visual access to the entire line.
  • Can address complex routing or multiple issues.
  • More yard disruption but sometimes the only option.

Answers

Questions We Hear Often

Will replacing my water line tear up my whole yard?

Not necessarily; trenchless methods minimize excavation where the line's path and depth allow for it.

How do you find a leak in an underground line without digging first?

Acoustic leak detection equipment picks up the sound of water escaping under pressure and narrows the search before any digging starts.

How long does a main line replacement take?

Trenchless replacement is often completed in a single day; traditional trenching can take longer depending on the line's length.

Is low water pressure always a main line problem?

Not always, a fixture-specific pressure drop is usually isolated, but a house-wide drop is a strong indicator of a main line issue.
